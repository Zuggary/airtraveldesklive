How rarity works

★ Legendary planes are on our watchlist: Dreamlifters, Belugas, bombers, spy planes and hurricane hunters. ◆ Epic types show up on our map only a handful of times. Gold solid outline = legendary. Purple dashed outline = epic. A plane counts once we see its type on the live map. The An-225 (destroyed 2022) and SR-71 (retired) stay on the list as history; they do not count toward your progress. We track planes on the live ADS-B map of the stream area (US airspace near our airports). Registration means the tail number painted on the plane.
