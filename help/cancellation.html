What does a flight cancellation mean?

A cancellation means the airline is not operating a scheduled flight. It is different from an airport-wide Ground Stop or Ground Delay Program. An FAA program alone does not establish that a flight is cancelled.

Check your flight with your airline

Arrivals, departures and your flight

For arrivals, a cancelled inbound flight does not arrive as scheduled. For departures, a cancelled outbound flight does not depart as scheduled. For a single flight, confirm the cancellation and available alternatives with the airline; an airport disruption does not mean every flight is cancelled.

What you can do now

  • Check the airline’s own status page through the airlines index.
  • Ask the airline for rebooking before paying for another ticket, meal or hotel. Ask what it will authorize in writing.
  • Keep receipts and the airline’s messages for any claim.

What is not known

This page cannot establish when a disruption ends, when your flight departs or arrives, or whether a connection works. A published program end time can change. Missing or stale data never means clear. Check directly with the airline.

Statutory refund rule

Refunds are a separate rule, 14 CFR 260.6, and it applies whatever the cause of the delay or cancellation.

If the airline cancels and you choose not to travel or accept a voucher or credit, DOT says you can receive a ticket refund.

Voluntary airline commitments

Meals, hotels and rebooking assistance are separate from the statutory ticket refund. The U.S. DOT Airline Cancellation and Delay Dashboard lists airline commitments for controllable disruptions. Eligibility depends on the airline’s terms and the circumstances. You may be owed assistance; ask the airline before paying. See the airlines index for individual commitments.
