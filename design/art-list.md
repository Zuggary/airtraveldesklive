# Art list (AN38)

Style for every still: minimal stylised digital painting of an airfield or terminal, deep navy darkness, sign-yellow window glow, emerald green lights. Status colour is the only strong colour: amber for delay, red for ground stop and closure. No text, no people, no tail numbers. The pictures show a kind of scene, never a real airport or event.
