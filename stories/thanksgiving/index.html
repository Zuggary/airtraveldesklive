Thanksgiving flying is running 3.8% above last year

TSA is screening more people than on the same days last year, and the busiest days are still ahead.

Air travel +3.8% in the seven days ending Thu Nov 19, 2026 versus the same seven days in 2025 (Nov 14 to 20). Latest TSA count: Thu Nov 19. TSA has not posted later days yet.

The short version

  • Busier than last year

    Yes. 2.61M people went through TSA checkpoints on Thu Nov 19, +4.6% on the same weekday last year. The seven-day change is +3.8%.

  • Which days to expect crowds

    Plan for crowds on Wed Nov 25 and Sun Nov 29, and a quiet Thanksgiving Day (Thu Nov 26). Last year: 2.75M the Wednesday before, 1.56M on Thanksgiving Day, and a record 3.13M the Sunday after (Nov 30, 2025).

  • Airports already struggling

    San Diego, Miami and LaGuardia have had the most delays: 26, 22, 19 hours with a delay or ground stop in the last 72 hours.

Method note & source ledger

Weekday alignment: Thanksgiving falls on the fourth Thursday of November (Nov 26, 2026). Every prior year is aligned by day offset (T-11 to T+4) so Saturday meets Saturday and Sunday meets Sunday.

Self-updating build: On every site build, tools/build_stories.py fetches the latest TSA daily table, merges cached historical years, reads the stored FAA hourly archive, and embeds a machine-readable trace block linking every displayed figure to its source file and field. Unpublished future dates remain empty slots rather than zero bars.

Canonical codes & closures: FAA readings merge legacy PBI rows into DJT and display SNA's multi-day closure listing from readings/state.json separately below the operational delay ranking.
