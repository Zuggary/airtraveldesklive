Privacy

Air Travel Desk sets no cookies of its own, has no accounts, and counts visits without cookies (see Visit counts, below). The site caches its own files on your device so it works offline; nothing you type is stored or sent. The optional YouTube (Google) video is the one exception, below.

Saved and last-viewed airports: when you save an airport or open one on the home page, its three-letter code is kept in your browser's local storage, on your device only, so the home page can show it again. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Clear it in your browser settings, or press "Remove from my airports".

What this page loads

The page loads only files hosted on this site: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, icons, the map outline, the radar image, and JSON data files. It does not load third-party fonts, images or embeds. The one third-party script is the Cloudflare visit counter, described under Visit counts. Searching for an airport happens in your browser; what you type is not sent anywhere.

Visit counts

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits. It sets no cookie and keeps nothing in your browser. Cloudflare says it does not fingerprint visitors. It sees the page address without anything after a question mark, the site you came from, your country, your browser, your operating system, your device type and how fast the page loaded. To make the request, your browser shows Cloudflare your IP address, as it does to any web server. It does not see anything you type. When you click an e-mail link or a link to one of a few other sites, the page also sends Cloudflare one fixed word for that click, such as out-mail or out-faa. It never sends the link address. We see only totals. We cannot see which visitor did what, and nothing follows you from one visit to the next.

Hosting logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs that may include your IP address and request metadata. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Flight and weather data

The data comes from FAA NAS status, National Weather Service alerts, the NOAA MRMS radar image, TSA checkpoint numbers, Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS) on-time numbers and adsb.lol (aircraft positions for map trails, a count of aircraft near airports and a note when several aircraft look to be holding, ODbL licence), aviationweather.gov (airport weather reports and forecasts, and convective SIGMETs), the NOAA Storm Prediction Center (storm outlooks) and the NOAA National Hurricane Center (active storms). An automated job fetches those public feeds about every 5 minutes and writes static files to this site, so loading this page sends no viewer data to any of them.

Aircraft data: adsb.lol, licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. The aircraft file we publish on this site (data/national.json: position, altitude, speed, heading and a short trail for each aircraft, refreshed by the automated job) is a database extract that we offer under the same ODbL 1.0 licence. Please credit adsb.lol if you reuse it. Our own hourly counts are not published.

Hotels and car rental places near each airport come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors) through the Overpass API. The automated job asks for them about once a week and saves the result as a static file on this site. Your browser never contacts OpenStreetMap or Overpass. Place websites and airport wait-time pages are plain links; they go to other sites only if you open them. The airline commitments table is a copy of the U.S. Department of Transportation's airline dashboard. Airport guide links come from the airports' own websites and are saved as a static file on this site; your browser does not contact them unless you open a link.

Aircraft data: adsb.lol (ODbL).

Live video: the home page has a "Load live video" button. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press it. When you press it, your browser loads YouTube’s player from www.youtube.com. YouTube is operated by Google; it receives your IP address and can set cookies under its own privacy notice. This site never sees what YouTube (Google) collects.

External links

The links to YouTube, the U.S. Department of Transportation, hotel and car rental websites, airport wait-time pages, Downtime Credit and the Lantern Drift game go to other websites that operate under their own privacy policies. The Downtime Credit links add a tag naming this site (utm_source=airtraveldesklive) so that site can count visits sent from here; the tag carries nothing about you.

Ads

This site is free and may show ads from Google AdSense on its guide pages. Ads pay for the site. They do not change what the pages show, and no advertiser sees what you type. The live board, the map and TV mode never show ads. This site shows no ads today.

If ads start, Google and its ad partners may use cookies and similar technology to show ads based on your visits to this site and other sites. Google shows its own consent message to visitors where the law requires one. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can turn off personalised ads in Google's ad settings, or opt out of personalised ads from other providers at aboutads.info.

Ad cookies come from Google, not from this site. The ads.txt file on this site lists the sellers allowed to sell its ad space.
