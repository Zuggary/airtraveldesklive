Privacy

Air Travel Desk sets no cookies of its own, runs no analytics or trackers, and has no accounts. The site caches its own files on your device so it works offline; nothing you type is stored or sent. The optional Twitch video is the one exception, below.

What this page loads

The page loads only files hosted on this site: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, icons, the map outline, the radar image, and JSON data files. It does not load third-party scripts, fonts, images or embeds. Searching for an airport happens in your browser; what you type is not sent anywhere.

Hosting logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs that may include your IP address and request metadata. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Flight and weather data

The data comes from FAA NAS status, National Weather Service alerts, the NOAA MRMS radar image, TSA checkpoint numbers, Bureau of Transportation Statistics (BTS) on-time numbers and adsb.lol (aircraft positions for map trails, a count of aircraft near airports and a note when several aircraft look to be holding, ODbL licence), aviationweather.gov (airport weather reports and forecasts, and convective SIGMETs), the NOAA Storm Prediction Center (storm outlooks) and the NOAA National Hurricane Center (active storms). An automated job fetches those public feeds about every 5 minutes and writes static files to this site, so loading this page sends no viewer data to any of them.

Aircraft data: adsb.lol, licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. The aircraft file we publish on this site (data/national.json: position, altitude, speed, heading and a short trail for each aircraft, refreshed by the automated job) is a database extract that we offer under the same ODbL 1.0 licence. Please credit adsb.lol if you reuse it. Our own hourly counts are not published.

Hotels and car rental places near each airport come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors) through the Overpass API. The automated job asks for them about once a week and saves the result as a static file on this site. Your browser never contacts OpenStreetMap or Overpass. Place websites and airport wait-time pages are plain links; they go to other sites only if you open them. The airline commitments table is a copy of the U.S. Department of Transportation's airline dashboard. Airport guide links come from the airports' own websites and are saved as a static file on this site; your browser does not contact them unless you open a link.

Aircraft data: adsb.lol (ODbL).

Live video: the home page has a "Load live video" button. Nothing is loaded from Twitch until you press it. When you press it, your browser loads Twitch's player from player.twitch.tv. Twitch is a separate company; it receives your IP address and can set cookies under its own privacy notice. This site never sees what Twitch collects.

External links

The links to Twitch, the U.S. Department of Transportation, hotel and car rental websites, airport wait-time pages and Downtime Credit go to other websites that operate under their own privacy policies. The Downtime Credit links add a tag naming this site (utm_source=airtraveldesklive) so that site can count visits sent from here; the tag carries nothing about you.

Contact

If you have a question or privacy concern, please open an issue on GitHub.
