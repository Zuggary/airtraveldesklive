This page lists every feed the site reads. The site checks each feed about every 5 minutes. If a feed is down, the site keeps the last good report and says so.

This site is automated and may be wrong or late. Check the official source or your airline before you travel.

Not used

  • Airline websites and apps: not read. Airline status pages are linked, never copied.
  • Paid flight-tracking sites (FlightAware, Flightradar24): their terms do not allow this use.
  • FAA SWIM and other login-only aviation feeds: not used.
  • Reader reports, social posts and news articles: never copied or used for a status.
  • Ad scripts and tracking pixels: none on this site. The only analytics is Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies (see the privacy page).

Limits of the data

  • Delays shown here are what the FAA NAS status feed lists, not every individual flight delay.
  • Aircraft positions are a sample with a delay, not a real-time radar feed.
  • A time in this table is when the site last had good data from that feed, not when the agency last updated it.
  • This site is not for flight dispatch or navigation.
